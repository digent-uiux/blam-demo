The four-step evening routine our team swears by

Cleanse, treat, seal, sleep. How to layer a serum and an oil without pilling, and why the order matters more than the number of products.

Mara EllisSkin therapist

Most of us own more skincare than we use. The good news: a calm, effective evening routine needs four steps, and none of them take longer than a minute. What changes the result is the order, and giving each layer a moment to settle.

Step 1: Cleanse twice, gently

If you wore SPF or makeup, start with a balm or oil cleanser on dry skin, then follow with a gel cleanser on damp skin. Use lukewarm water: hot water strips the barrier you are about to feed.

Step 2: Treat with one active

Pick one active per night and rotate through the week. Layering several at once is the fastest way to irritation.

  • Monday and Thursday: a retinal serum, two pumps.
  • Tuesday and Friday: a lactic acid toner on a cotton pad.
  • The other nights: a niacinamide serum to rest and repair.

“Wait sixty seconds between layers. Press the product in, don't rub.”

Mara Ellis, skin therapist

Step 3: Seal with an oil or cream

Oil goes last because it slows water loss from every layer underneath. Warm three drops between your palms and press them over the face and neck. Dry skin can add a cream first, then the oil on top.

Step 4: Sleep on it

Change your pillowcase twice a week and sleep on your back if you can. Your routine works overnight; the fewer things rubbing against your face, the better it works.
