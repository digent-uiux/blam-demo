The apothecary

Four things we hold to

  • No. I

    Three farms, by name

    Oat, olive and calendula from growers we visit each harvest. The farm is printed under every lot number.

  • No. II

    Small batches

    Forty jars at a time, poured and dated by hand, so nothing sits in a warehouse for a year.

  • No. III

    Nothing to hide

    Full formulas, with percentages, on the label and online. No fragrance blends, no fillers.

  • No. IV

    Glass, refilled

    Every jar comes back for a refill or is washed and reused. Return five, the sixth is on us.
