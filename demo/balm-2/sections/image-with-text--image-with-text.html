Why we still grow our own calendula.

Dear reader,

Twelve years ago I had eczema on both hands and a cupboard of creams that stung. I started infusing calendula petals in sunflower oil on my kitchen table, because it was the one thing my grandmother swore by. It worked well enough that friends began to ask for jars.

We are fourteen people now, with a small lab and a half-acre walled garden. From June to September we pick the calendula every third morning, when the heads are fully open, and steep them for six weeks before a single gram goes into a formula.

With care,

Margot, founder and head formulator

P.S. The calendula balm from this summer's harvest is batch 26-07. It's the one I keep by the sink.
