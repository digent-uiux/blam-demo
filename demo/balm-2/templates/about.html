Why we still grow our own calendula.

Dear reader,

Twelve years ago I had eczema on both hands and a cupboard of creams that stung. I started infusing calendula petals in sunflower oil on my kitchen table, because it was the one thing my grandmother swore by. It worked well enough that friends began to ask for jars.

We are fourteen people now, with a small lab and a half-acre walled garden. From June to September we pick the calendula every third morning, when the heads are fully open, and steep them for six weeks before a single gram goes into a formula.

With care,

Margot, founder and head formulator

P.S. The calendula balm from this summer's harvest is batch 26-07. It's the one I keep by the sink.

How we count +

Natural origin follows ISO 16128 and is weighed per batch, then averaged across formulas. Customers are unique delivery addresses, not accounts. Figures are checked every January against the batch book.

Four people, one slow apothecary

Every formula is written, grown, tested and kept by the same four people. Open a shelf note to see what each of us reaches for.

  • 01 · Head chemist

    Iris

    Writes every formula on paper first, then takes out half the ingredients.

    Iris is never without the Oat Milk Cleansing Balm. She reformulated it four times to drop the emulsifier.

    Oat Milk Cleansing Balm · $42

  • 02 · Botanist

    Efua

    Walks the olive and calendula farms each spring before we sign for a harvest.

    Efua presses a flower from every farm she visits into the batch book. The calendula comes from a hillside two hours north.

    Calendula Calm Drops · $56

  • 03 · Clinical esthetician

    Nadia

    Tests each balm on her own clients for six weeks before it gets a name.

    Nadia keeps a skin diary for every tester. If three people flush, the formula goes back to Iris.

    Olive Leaf Night Cream · $64

  • 04 · Quality & stability

    Walter

    Keeps a jar from every batch in the cool room for three full years.

    Walter opens an old jar each Friday to check scent and texture. Nothing we sell has gone off on his watch.
