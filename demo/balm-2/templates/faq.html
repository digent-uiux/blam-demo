Shipping & returns

This policy explains how we ship your order, what it costs and how to send something back. It applies to every skincare, makeup, hair and body order placed on Lumen.

§ 01Order processing

Orders placed before 2pm ET on a business day leave our warehouse the same day. Orders placed after 2pm, on weekends or on public holidays ship the next business day.

You'll get a confirmation email as soon as your order is placed and a second email with a tracking link once it ships.

§ 02Shipping rates & times

Delivery times are counted in business days from the day your order ships.

  • Standard (3–5 days): $6, free on orders over $75
  • Express (1–2 days): $15
  • Next day (order by noon): $12, mainland US only

§ 03International orders

We ship to more than 40 countries. Duties and taxes are calculated at checkout and included in your total, so there's nothing extra to pay on delivery.

Some items, such as aerosol dry shampoo, nail polish and fragrance, can't ship by air outside the US. They're marked on the product page.

§ 04Returns

You can return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Items must be unopened, with the seal intact and in their original box.

Items we can't take back

Opened skincare, makeup and hair care, used tools such as brushes and gua sha, and final-sale sets are not returnable, for hygiene reasons. If a product irritates your skin, contact us and we'll find a fix.

How to start a return

Go to Orders in your account, choose the items and print the prepaid label. Drop the parcel at any carrier location within 14 days.

§ 05Refunds

Once your return reaches us, we inspect it within 3 business days and refund the original payment method. Your bank may take a further 5–7 days to show the credit.

Original shipping costs are refunded only when the item arrived damaged or we sent the wrong item.

§ 06Damaged or missing items

If something arrives damaged, send us a photo within 7 days of delivery. We'll send a replacement or refund it in full, and you won't need to return it.

§ 07Contact us

Questions about this policy? Email care@lumen.example or use live chat, Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm ET.

No. 01

Your skin type

  • Wash with a gentle cleanser, pat dry and wait an hour without applying anything. Tight all over means dry, shine on the nose and forehead only means combination, shine everywhere means oily. If it stings or reddens easily, read our sensitive range first, whatever your type.

  • Not usually. It is squalane and jojoba, both close to the skin's own sebum, so oily skin often produces less once it stops being stripped. Start with two drops at night only.

  • Keep the cleanser and serum all year, and swap only the last step: the whipped cream from spring to autumn, the balm when the heating comes on.
No. 02

Actives, in plain words

  • Yes. Bakuchiol is gentle enough to share a routine with vitamin C, though we still suggest vitamin C in the morning and bakuchiol at night so each has its own time to work.

  • We formulate with bakuchiol and rosehip oil, which smooth texture without the dryness many people get from retinol. If you already use a prescription retinoid, keep it and skip our bakuchiol serum.

  • The face range is fragrance-free. Only the body balms carry essential oils, under 0.5%, and every one is named on the label.
No. 03

Opened products & returns

  • Yes, within 45 days, even half used. If something doesn't agree with your skin, send it back and we refund in full or swap it for a different formula.

  • Look for the small open-jar symbol on the base: 6M means six months, 12M a year. Our balms are anhydrous, so they keep their full 12 months with clean, dry fingers or the spatula.

  • Opened returns can't be resold. Unopened ones go back to stock; opened ones are emptied and the glass and tins are cleaned and recycled.
No. 04

Orders & delivery

  • Orders before 1 pm leave the same working day. Standard delivery takes 2–4 days and is free over $60; next-day delivery is $9.

  • The jars are glass or aluminium, the lids are aluminium and the box is recycled card held with paper tape. The only plastic is the pump on the cleanser, which you can send back to us.
