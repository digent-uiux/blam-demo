Brewing

03 · 3

  • Whole bean keeps its aroma longest. If you have no grinder, pick your brewer at checkout and we grind it on the day it ships.

  • Best between 7 and 40 days after the roast date printed on the bag. Keep them sealed, cool and out of the light.

  • 1:16 for filter (15 g to 240 g of water at 94 °C) and 1:2 for espresso. Each bag carries its own recipe card.
