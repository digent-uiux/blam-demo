Six hundred and forty smallholders deliver ripe cherry to Hambela each morning in the season. Abebe Tadesse and his team float out the light fruit, then lay the rest whole on raised beds for three weeks, turning it every hour so it dries slow and even. That patience is the blueberry in your cup.
