Our story

Ten yearsin fourchapters.

From a market cart to a roastery in a boat shed, and still one batch at a time.

Swipe

2014

A cart and a borrowed grinder

Saturdays at the harbour market: one brewer, two origins and a queue that kept getting longer.

2017

Our first bar

Twelve seats on Kiln Street, a slow bar for filter and a wall of beans you could smell from the pavement.

2020

A roastery of our own

A 15 kg drum in an old boat shed. We started buying direct and publishing every price we pay.

2024

Tea joins the bar

Whole-leaf teas from three gardens, whisked matcha and the same small-batch care as the coffee.

How we buy

Good coffeestarts with a fairprice.

We buy fewer lots and pay more for them. Three rules keep us honest, and we publish the numbers so you can check.

  1. 01We visit every farmSomeone from the roastery stands on every farm we buy from, at least once every two harvests.
  2. 02We pay for the cupPrices are set by cup score, not the commodity market, and agreed before the harvest starts.
  3. 03We publish every priceWhat we paid, to whom and for how much: every lot, every year, in one open report.
