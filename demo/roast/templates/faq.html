Orders & shipping

01 · 4

  • We roast on Mondays and ship on Tuesdays. Order by Sunday 6 pm and your beans leave the roastery the day after roasting.

  • Free over $45 within the country. Below that it is a flat $5.50, tracked, and usually arrives in two to three days.

  • Yes, to most of Europe and North America. Rates and duties show at checkout before you pay.

  • Until Sunday 6 pm, yes: reply to your confirmation email or write to the bar. After that it is already in the roaster.

Subscriptions

02 · 3

  • From your account, under Subscription: skip the next bag, pause for up to three months, or change the cadence at any time.

  • Yes. Pick a new roast before the Sunday cut-off and the next bag follows it. The 15% club price stays.

  • No. Cancel whenever you like; we only ask for a one-line reason so we can get better.

Brewing

03 · 3

  • Whole bean keeps its aroma longest. If you have no grinder, pick your brewer at checkout and we grind it on the day it ships.

  • Best between 7 and 40 days after the roast date printed on the bag. Keep them sealed, cool and out of the light.

  • 1:16 for filter (15 g to 240 g of water at 94 °C) and 1:2 for espresso. Each bag carries its own recipe card.

Wholesale

04 · 2

  • Yes, from 5 kg a week. We include barista training, a brew audit and the same Monday roast.
